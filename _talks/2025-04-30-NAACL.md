This talk presents findings from our NAACL/HLT 2025 paper, which applies a text-based causal inference framework—enhanced CausalBERT—to estimate the isolated impact of specific review aspects on overall school ratings, addressing confounding through calibration, tuning, and interpretability techniques.

To illustrate our methods and results, a detailed PowerPoint presentation has been prepared, which outlines the study's background, the data analysis process, and key insights gained:
